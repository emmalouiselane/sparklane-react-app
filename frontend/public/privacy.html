Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Spark Lane Dev ("we", "us", or "our") operates Sparklane Personal Assistant (the "Service"). This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we use it, how it is shared, and the choices available to you.

Who is responsible for your information?

Spark Lane Dev is the controller of the personal information processed through the Service. For privacy questions or requests, contact emma@sparklane.dev.

Information we collect

  • Google account information: Google account identifier, name, email address, profile photo, and other profile fields returned by Google when you sign in.
  • Google Calendar information: calendar events that you ask the Service to display, and event details you ask the Service to create. The Service requests Google profile, email, calendar, and calendar-events permissions.
  • Information you enter: time logs, to-do items, budget settings and payments, and other information you choose to enter into the Service.
  • Authentication and technical information: encrypted Google access and refresh tokens, session identifiers, session expiry information, and basic request logs such as IP address, browser, and request time that may be recorded by our hosting and security systems.
  • Cookies: the Service uses a necessary, HTTP-only session cookie to keep you signed in and protect your account. We do not use this cookie for advertising.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate you, provide the Service's calendar, time-log, to-do, and budgeting features, save the information you ask us to save, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, communicate with you about the Service, and comply with legal obligations.

Where UK data-protection law applies, our intended legal bases are performance of our agreement with you, your consent for optional Google Calendar access, our legitimate interests in security and service administration, and compliance with legal obligations where applicable.

Google API data and limited use

We use information received from Google APIs only to provide the Google-connected features that are visible in the Service and as otherwise permitted by Google's policies. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, credit assessment, or unrelated purposes. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share or make information available to:

  • Google: when the Service uses Google Sign-In or Google Calendar on your behalf.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: including database, application-hosting, logging, and security providers that process information only to operate and protect the Service.
  • Authorities or advisers: where required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger or sale.

Providers may process information in countries outside your own. Where required, we use appropriate legal safeguards for international transfers.

How long we keep information

The Service is provided free of charge. We keep account and user-entered information while your account is active and for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, maintain security, or meet legal obligations. Session records expire automatically. Google tokens are retained while needed for the calendar connection and are removed when the account or connection is removed, subject to technical and legal retention requirements. You are responsible for the information you choose to upload or enter and should keep your own copies of anything important.

Your choices and rights

You can stop granting Google Calendar access through your Google account settings, stop using the Service, and ask us to access, correct, export, or delete personal information we hold about you. You may also have rights to restrict or object to processing and to complain to your local data-protection authority. To request removal of your account or any information you have uploaded or entered, contact emma@sparklane.dev with enough detail for us to identify the information. We may need to verify your identity and may retain limited information where the law permits or requires it.

Security

We use measures designed to protect information, including authenticated access, HTTP-only session cookies, access controls, and encryption for stored Google tokens. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Age requirement

The Service is intended only for individuals aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly provide the Service to anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Service or our processing changes. We will update the date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to emma@sparklane.dev.
